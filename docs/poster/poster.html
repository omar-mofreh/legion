01 · WHYHealth data should lead to better care.

The problem المشكلة

Data in many places

Pulse, sleep, steps and oxygen live in different apps and records. Patients struggle to see the whole picture, and clinicians start every visit without context.

Our solution الحل

One connected picture

Legion brings biometrics, AI-assisted guidance and care teams into one workspace. You see clear trends, and your clinicians get a focused summary before the conversation starts.
